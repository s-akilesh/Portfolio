DEFINING PROBLEM

What is Flyer Eats

A flexible meal scheduling for PG residents and professionals, enabling users to customize plans, track orders in real time, and avoid payment waste. It simplifies food delivery with on-demand options and eliminates fixed meal timing hassles.

Problem Ststement

Users face challenges in finding affordable, healthy, and flexible meal options that suit their daily routines. Existing solutions such as PG meals and online food delivery services are often inflexible, repetitive, or costly, and do not support customized meal preferences. There is a lack of a convenient platform that allows users to order, schedule, and manage their meals easily while ensuring variety, consistency, and timely delivery.

RESEARCH & ANALYSYS

User Interview

5 days

Conducted 5 days of online interviews, observing real user behavior across planning and delivery phases.

22 Participants

Interviewed over 22 users to understand their needs and challenges.

Difficulty in finding nearby food options with good quality and consistency.

Existing PG or hostel meal plans are inflexible and often lead to wastage.

High cost of ordering food regularly through online delivery platforms.

Lack of customizable meal options to match personal preferences or diet plans.

No easy way to schedule or manage daily meals across different locations.

Limited time and facilities for self-cooking, especially for those with busy routines.

Repetitive menus and limited variety in available food options.

Inconsistent or delayed delivery experiences from existing providers.

Ideation

User research revealed major gaps in the current food ordering experience - limited meal flexibility, lack of personalization, and no option to plan meals in advance.
Our ideation phase focused on bridging these gaps by addressing user needs (convenience, customization, affordability, and planning), business needs (order consistency and multi-branch management), and market gaps (absence of a unified food planning and catering platform).

The brainstorming sessions led to solutions centered around a food planner that allows users to schedule their meals in advance, customize diet preferences, and manage deliveries easily.
Additionally, features like branch-level order tracking, flexible meal subscriptions, and smart recommendations were ideated to create a seamless, personalized, and efficient food experience for every user.

User Flow

Through defined user goals and pain points, we mapped a user flow that ensures a smooth, flexible, and intuitive food ordering experience

Design

Usability testing

Usability testing was conducted with a group of potential users representing the target audience to evaluate the overall app flow and user experience.

During the first testing round, 8 users found the food planner screen slightly confusing, particularly when selecting or customizing their meals.
Based on their feedback, the flow was restructured to make the planning process more straightforward and visually clear.

An A/B testing session was then conducted specifically for the food planner screen, comparing Version 1 (initial design) and Version 2 (refined design).
The goal was to determine which version provided a simpler and faster meal planning experience.

Results showed that Version 2 performed better users completed tasks faster and expressed higher confidence while selecting meals.
The improved layout, clear categorization, and guided selection process reduced confusion and hesitation.

Feedback across the rest of the app was overwhelmingly positive. The simplified item selection, clearer navigation, and streamlined ordering process significantly reduced confusion and hesitation, especially for bulk and catering orders.
